Restrictions

Reservation includes 2 adults, 2 children, 1 pet, 2 vehicles (RV + one tow vehicle).

  • Additional person: $5 (Max 6 adults or 8 people / site per month)
  • Additional car: $15 (Max 3 vehicles per month)
  • Tents & Fires permitted only in designated sites.
💳

Payment Due at Booking

  • Ongoing reservations pay the first month, a security deposit, and reservation fee prior to check-in.
  • Guests must keep a credit card on file for damages.
  • No cash or personal checks accepted.
🔄

Cancellation Policy

  • Booking fees and credit card processing fees paid to third parties are non-refundable.
  • Reservations cancelled > 1 month before arrival date receive a full refund (minus $25 processing fee & credit card fees).
  • Cancellations 1 month to 10 days before arrival receive a 50% refund.
  • No refunds for cancellations within 10 days of arrival or after check-in.
