Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

Check-In & Check-Out Times

Check-In: After 4:00 PM on the day your reservation begins.
Check-Out: By 10:00 AM on your day of departure.
Early check-in and late check-out options are available for a small fee upon request, subject to availability.

Gate Access & Wi-Fi Codes

The day before your scheduled arrival date, we automatically send you an email containing your personal security codes to open the main entrance gate, access the climate-controlled restrooms & laundry, and connect to our high-speed 1 GB fiber Wi-Fi network.

Site Restrictions & Occupancy

Each standard reservation includes 2 adults, 2 children, 1 pet, and 2 vehicles (RV + one passenger vehicle).

  • Additional Person: $5 per stay (Max 6 adults or 8 total people per site).
  • Additional Vehicle: $15 per stay (Max 3 vehicles per site).
  • Tents & Campfires: Permitted only on select wide, interior sites (Sites 13, 21, 22, 23, 31, 39, 40, 41, 43, 44, 53).

Credit & Debit Card Payments

A standard 2.9% card processing fee is added to credit and debit card transactions. A valid credit card must remain on file with management for damages and incidentals.

Cancellation Policy

  • Card processing and third-party booking fees are non-refundable.
  • 30+ Days Before Arrival: Full refund minus processing fees.
  • 10–30 Days Before Arrival: 50% refund minus processing fees.
  • Within 10 Days or After Check-In: Non-refundable.

Park Rules & Conduct

All guests and visitors in your party must follow park rules at all times. Management reserves the right to eject any party without refund for unruly or unsafe behavior. Review our complete rules on the Park Policies page.
