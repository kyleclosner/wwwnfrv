## **Restrictions**

Reservation includes 2 adults, 2 children, 1 pet, 2 vehicles (RV \+ one tow vehicle).&nbsp;

* Additional person: $5.  Max 6 adults or 8 people / site.  (per month)  
* Additional car:  $15.  (per month). Max 3 vehicles.&nbsp;  
* Tents & Fires permitted only in designated sites.

## **Payment due at Booking, before Check-In**

* Ongoing reservations pay the first month, a security deposit, and reservation fee.  
* Guests must keep a credit card on file for damages.&nbsp;&nbsp;  
* No cash or personal checks.

## **Cancellation**

* Booking fees and card processing fees paid to third parties are non-refundable.  
* Reservations cancelled more than one month before the arrival date will receive a full refund.  
* Canceling one month to 10 days before the arrival date, will receive a 50% refund.&nbsp;&nbsp;  
* We cannot offer refunds for cancellations within 10 days of arrival, nor after check in.  
* Monthly reservations canceled more than one month from the arrival date will receive 50% of their deposit.  Cancelations made within one month of arrival will forfeit their deposit.  
* All cancellations charge a $25 processing fee, and credit card fees
