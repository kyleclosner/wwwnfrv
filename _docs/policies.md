# Campground Policies	![][image1]

Noble Forest RV (“Campground”) // 22833 FM 1774, Plantersville TX 77363 // (936) 894-2079

Revised: Sep 19, 2026

## Rules & Regulations

### **Check-In**

1. User and all guests must check-in at the park office.&nbsp;&nbsp;  
2. User will provide a copy of Driver’s License at check-in.

### **Check-Out**

1. Check out time is 11 AM  
2. Move Out Notice for weekly / monthly reservations.  14-day written notice is required prior to departure.  If less than 14 days' notice is provided, Noble Forest RV will deduct the "missing" notice days from your deposit, at the prorated rate.  
3. No refunds for early departures.

### **General Conduct & Appearance**

1. Keep your RV, site, and personal belongings neat and orderly at all times.  
2. All RVs must be kept in good condition and free from excessive dirt, mildew, or visible exterior damage. To maintain park aesthetics and guest experience, management reserves the right to require that RVs be cleaned if they become unsightly. If not addressed after notice, the park may arrange for professional cleaning at the guest’s expense.  
3. Guests are free to wash their own RV onsite, so long as the hose is equipped with a nozzle or sprayer so that the water is not permitted to free flow.  
4. Noble Forest RV reserves the right to refuse or terminate service based on the appearance or condition of your RV or site.  
5. Sites must be kept free of debris, excessive storage, or clutter.  
6. Personal behavior that disturbs other guests will not be tolerated.

### **Payments**

1. Payment method.  We accept rent payments in money orders, credit cards, and debit cards.  No personal checks or cash.  
2. User shall pay Campground the rate stated in the reservation, for use of the RV site.  This rate shall be paid in advance and without demand. The rate for use of the RV Site may be increased by giving advance notice to User.&nbsp;&nbsp;  
3. Overdue Rate. If, on the day after the due date, User has not paid the total amount due under the terms of this Agreement, the rate will convert to the currently advertised daily rate for the site.  User will pay Campground interest on the outstanding amount at an annual interest rate of 18%. Campground will not accept partial payments.  
4. User will provide Campground with information for a valid credit card to keep on file. The credit card information will be used by Campground in the event that Campground must charge User for cleaning or repairing the Site, or if User vacates without paying Campground the total amount due under this Agreement.  
5. Violations. Campground may assess fees to the User for violating park rules.  Manager may issue a warning, at their discretion.  Fee schedule is available in the park office.

### **Electric Utility**

1. Electric Utility included in daily rate.  Weekly / Monthly / Extended stay User shall pay Campground, all electrical power charges used by User in connection with the use of the RV Site. This amount is calculated by Campground according to TEX. UTIL. CODE § 184.034. The beginning meter reading will be taken at User check-in.  If, at any time during the term of this Agreement, User becomes delinquent in the payment of electric services, Campground shall withhold electric services until User pays the delinquent amount, pursuant to TEX. UTIL. CODE § 184.036.  
2. Excess Electric.  All-inclusive Sites cap electricity consumption at an average of 20 kWh per day or $80 per month.  Excess electricity consumption will be billed to the User at the rate calculated above.

### **Noise & Quiet Hours**

1. **Quiet Hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.**  
2. Loud music, parties, or disruptive noise are not permitted at any time.&nbsp;  
3. No generators may be used in Noble Forest RV.  
4. Anyone under the age of 18 may not leave their Site during Quiet Time unless it is to and from the bathhouse.  
5. Please be courteous to other guests at all times.

&nbsp;

### **Site Appearance**

1. Outdoor furniture and BBQ grills must be neatly organized, in good condition, and uniform.  
2. Mats and satellite dishes must remain on your designated RV pad.  
3. Exercise equipment, indoor furniture, clotheslines, or hanging laundry is not allowed.  
4. No clutter or items stored under the RV. Totes are allowed onsite, but they must be neatly organized and stacked, lids closed, in clean condition and uniform in color.  
5. Window A/C units are not permitted  
6. Flag Policy.  The following flags are allowed to be displayed at your site and throughout the property, American, Canadian, Military, including Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Coast Guard, POW, MIA, and veterans, sports, and decorative flags such as holiday, seasonal or garden. Political or other promotional flags/signs are not permitted  
7. Pet enclosures are allowed on a case-by-case basis. Contact the onsite manager for approval.  
8. No trash or trash cans are allowed outside of the RV. Place trash in our onsite dumpsters.  
9. All items in grass areas must be moved on mowing days.

### **Site Setup & Utilities**

1. Use proper leveling blocks only.  
2. Water pressure regulators are strongly recommended. Leaking connections must be shutoff until they can be replaced.  
3. Sewer hoses must be kept off the ground using an accordion-style support, with a proper sewer connection (sewer collar/donut) to prevent gas escape. Leaking sewer hoses must be replaced immediately.  
4. Continuous sewer flush connections are not allowed. Black tank must remain closed except when dumping.

### **Vehicles**

1. Limit of 2 vehicles per RV Site.  
2. Additional vehicles must be parked in storage sites for a fee.  
3. All vehicles must be operational, and carry current insurance and registration.

### **Safety**

1. Children must be always supervised by an adult.  
2. This property uses an electrically powered gate.  Pedestrians may be crushed by moving gates.  Pedestrians must use the pedestrian entrance.  Campground is not liable for damage or injury to persons, vehicles, pets, or property, caused by the gate.

### &nbsp;**Fires**

1. All open fires must be contained in a fire ring installed by Noble Forest RV Village, or in an elevated fire pit.  
2. Small charcoal or gas grills are allowed.  
3. Fires must be attended at all times.  If your fire is hot to the touch, User must be outside with it.  
4. Fireworks of any kind are prohibited.  
5. In the event of a burn ban issued by Grimes county, no wood fires are allowed.

### **Pets**

1. Pets must be on a leash or carrier at all times when outside your RV or dog park.  
2. No pets allowed inside the buildings.  
3. No unattended pets.  If the pet is outside, the User must be outside with it.  
4. Clean up after your pets immediately — this includes all grassy areas and common areas.  
5. Excessive barking, aggressive behavior, or nuisance behavior will not be tolerated. Management reserves the right to require the removal of any disruptive or aggressive pet.  
6. User is liable for any damage or injury caused by his or her pet.  
7. Maximum of 3 pets per site.

### **Wifi & Internet**

1. Complimentary wifi is provided throughout the park for basic web browsing, email, and light internet use.  
2. Do not add “repeaters” or network extenders to the wifi network  
3. Our wifi is not designed to support gaming, large downloads, or other high-bandwidth activities.  
4. Guests who require high-speed internet for work or entertainment should plan to use their own cellular hotspot or arrange for a private internet connection.  
5. Noble Forest RV cannot guarantee uninterrupted or high-speed wifi service and is not responsible for technical issues beyond our control.

### **Background Check**

1. User and all guests authorize Campground to perform checks, including criminal and credit checks, in connection with their use of Campground’s property. Campground shall keep information provided confidential. User certifies that the information provided is true and accurate to the best of their knowledge.

### **Right to Terminate**

1. Campground’s Right to Terminate Agreement. Campground may terminate this Agreement for any or no reason by providing User a notice for User to leave the RV Site (“Agreement Termination Notice”). User agrees to render peaceful possession of the RV Site to Campground within three (3) days of the date provided on the Agreement Termination Notice. The three (3) days’ notice shall not be required if termination is the result of an Act of Default. Upon User’s peaceful surrender of the RV Site to Campground, Campground will refund to User the proportionate amount of any unused amounts previously paid by User to Campground.  
2. Early Termination by User. Campground may recover as damages for User’s early termination of this Agreement an amount equal to the amount that remains outstanding for the term of this Agreement  
3. Default by User. The following acts constitute defaults by User (“Acts of Default”):  
   1. Failing to timely pay the RV Site use rate,or other lawful charges when due under this Agreement;  
   2. Giving false information to Campground;  
   3. User, User’s guests and/or occupants failing to comply with this Agreement, such as violating provisions of this Agreement or committing serious misconduct or criminal acts;  
   4. Remaining on the Property after giving notice of termination and intent to vacate; and/or&nbsp;  
   5. Remaining on the Property after Campground gave notice of termination at the end of the term or an Agreement Termination Notice.

## Cancellation Policy

We understand that life happens, and you can’t always predict your plans.  We ask our guests to understand that canceling reservations places a significant burden on our small business.  We strive to make refunds when possible.

1. Booking fees and card processing fees paid to third parties are non-refundable.  
2. Reservations cancelled more than one month before the arrival date will receive a full refund.  
3. Canceling one month to 10 days before the arrival date, will receive a 50% refund.&nbsp;&nbsp;  
4. We cannot offer refunds for cancellations within 10 days of arrival, nor after User checks in.  
5. Monthly reservations canceled more than one month from the arrival date will receive 50% of their deposit.  Cancelations made withing one month of arrival will forfeit their deposit.  
6. All cancellations charge a $25 processing fee, and credit card fees.
